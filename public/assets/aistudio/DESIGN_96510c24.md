## Brand & Style

The brand visualizes architectural clarity, precision structural glazing, and bespoke interior space creation. It addresses an audience of interior architects, luxury home developers, and high-end residential owners who view glass, mirrors, and custom partitions not merely as building materials, but as light-sculpting architectural features.

The design language balances **Minimalism** with subtle **Glassmorphic Precision**:
- High-key ambient surfaces with expansive white space simulate natural skylight illumination.
- Micro-interactions emulate the pristine refraction and edge-polished transparency of tempered and laminated structural glass.
- Structural layout lines remain crisp, deliberate, and planar, reflecting contemporary structural engineering without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the optical characteristics of float glass, natural daylight, and architectural contrast:

- **Primary Canvas (`#FAFAF8`) & Secondary Plinth (`#F2F0EC`):** A warm off-white and limestone-inspired grey pair that prevents glare while highlighting transparent photography, render finishes, and glass tint treatments.
- **Deep Slate Ground (`#171B20`):** Replaces pure black to provide severe contrast for high-legibility typographic hierarchies and structural outlines.
- **Vibrant Glazing Cyan (`#00B4D8`) & Hover (`#0096B4`):** Represents edge-lit tempered glass, modern interior swimming pools, and high-performance solar coatings. Used strictly for directional focus, interactive highlights, and conversion triggers.
- **Petrol Navy Gradient Base (`#0A1E2F` to `#1E3A5F`):** Anchors terminal sections (case studies, technical engineering specifications, consultation intake, and footer), lending industrial authority and night-time structural ambiance.
- **Safety / Verification Accent (`#D71920`):** Reserved exclusively for technical compliance markers, security seals (e.g., ABNT/INMETRO tempered ratings), and critical warranty badges.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a singular, exceptionally versatile geometric sans-serif: **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines:** Set with high contrast, tighter kerning (`letterSpacing: -0.02em` to `-0.03em`), and tight vertical cadence to resemble architectural project monographs.
- **Body:** Open counters and a wide x-height guarantee effortless legibility when rendering dimensional specifications, glass thickness formulas (e.g., 8mm, 10mm, 6+6mm acoustic laminate), and project descriptions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with tracked uppercase weight to produce balanced, technical indexing labels (e.g., `TECH SPEC // TEMPERED EN-12150`).

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid governs editorial pacing and gallery structures:
- **Canvas Breakpoints:** Mobile (`< 768px`, 4 columns), Tablet (`768px – 1024px`, 8 columns), Desktop (`> 1024px`, 12 columns, max-width `1440px` centered).
- **Rhythm & Air:** Sections alternate between tight technical specification clusters (`space-md` to `space-lg`) and expansive architectural photography viewports framed by `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` section boundaries.
- **Horizontal Paneling:** Cards align with architectural mullions; column gutters maintain consistent structural gaps of `2rem` across full-resolution desktop displays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines tonal layering with translucent physical optical dynamics:

1. **Surface Tiers:**
   - Base canvas: `#FAFAF8`
   - Recessed or alternate architectural plane: `#F2F0EC`
   - Floating panels & drawers: `#FFFFFF` with glass edge definitions.
2. **Glassmorphism & Luster:**
   - Navigation bars, detail overlays, and modal viewports use `rgba(250, 250, 248, 0.78)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
   - Subtle perimeter rim light: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` on top and left borders, and `1px solid rgba(23, 27, 32, 0.06)` on bottom and right borders, mimicking beveled edge refraction.
3. **Ambient Lighting (Soft Architectural Shadows):**
   - **Level 1 (Resting Card / Module):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 27, 32, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 27, 32, 0.02)`
   - **Level 2 (Hover / Floating Showcase):** `0 16px 40px -6px rgba(23, 27, 32, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 27, 32, 0.03)`
   - **Level 3 (Interactive Modals / Material Selectors):** `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(10, 30, 47, 0.16)`

## Shapes

The form language references industrial CNC glass-cutting techniques and edge grinding:
- **Panels, Interactive Cards, & Dialogs:** Standardized corner geometry set at 12px to 16px (`rounded-lg` through `rounded-xl`), creating a modern, tactile feel without drifting into playful or cartoonish geometry.
- **Interactive Control Elements:** Buttons, inputs, and toggles adopt precise 8px corners (`rounded-md` equivalent to shape index 2), maintaining visual alignment with extruded aluminum frame systems.
- **Badges and Filters:** Strict pill forms for categorical tagging to contrast against planar card grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid vibrant cyan (`#00B4D8`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, 8px radius, horizontal padding `1.75rem`, vertical padding `0.875rem`. Hover state transitions to `#0096B4` accompanied by subtle expansion glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Architectural Ghost:** Border of `1px solid rgba(23, 27, 32, 0.15)`, background transparent, text `#171B20`. On hover: `background: #FFFFFF`, border color `#171B20`.
- **Inverted Terminal Action (Petrol Navy sections):** Translucent white surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`, hover turns to pure white background with `#0A1E2F` typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(23, 27, 32, 0.12)`, radius 8px, text `#171B20`. Focus states transition border to `#00B4D8` with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.15)`.
- **Select & Custom Spec Radios:** Segmented controls enclosed in `#F2F0EC` containers, active selection highlighted with a crisp white elevated pill tile.

### Product & Project Cards
- **Structure:** Clean 1px border using `rgba(23, 27, 32, 0.05)`, white surface background, 16px radius, internal padding `1.5rem`.
- **Media Window:** Aspect ratios locked strictly to 4:3 or 16:10 with corner radii of 10px inside the container. Micro-interaction yields a delicate image zoom (`scale(1.02)`) through an overflow-hidden mask.

### Badges & Technical Seals
- **Certification Marker:** Solid badge with background `#D71920`, pure white tracked typography (`label-sm`), 4px corner radius, signaling official glass safety ratings and technical standards.
- **Glass Spec Chip:** Translucent cyan surface (`rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.08)`), text `#0096B4`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.2)`.

### Glass Finish Configurator (Domain-Specific Component)
- High-precision swatch selector allowing clients to toggle between Extra-Clear Low-Iron, Fluted/Ribbed (Canelado), Acid-Etched (Jateado/Satinato), Smoked Grey (Fumê), and Bronze finishes with real-time refraction lighting previews.
